Fani-Kayode: Over 100 Nigerians Murdered in South Africa in Five Years

news

By Staff Reporter · September 13, 2026

Former minister Femi Fani-Kayode has raised alarm that more than 100 Nigerians have been murdered in South Africa within the past five years, calling the situation a national emergency.

Former Aviation Minister Femi Fani-Kayode has raised alarm that more than 100 Nigerians have been murdered in South Africa within the past five years, describing the ongoing xenophobic attacks as a national emergency that demands a firm diplomatic response.

Fani-Kayode, who made the claim on his social media accounts, said the figure included documented cases of targeted killings, mob violence and extrajudicial killings by private security operatives and members of the public. He accused the South African government of failing to protect Nigerian nationals and called on President Tinubu to consider recalling Nigeria's High Commissioner for consultations.

The statement came as the National Assembly voted to suspend parliamentary relations with South Africa and as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs summoned the South African High Commissioner in Abuja. Vice President Shettima had earlier said Nigeria would not tolerate the dehumanisation of its citizens anywhere in the world, linking the crisis to broader patterns of anti-African sentiment on the continent.
