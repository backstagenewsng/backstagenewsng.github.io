Police Arrest Suspect Behind OPay Shutdown Rumour That Caused Mass Withdrawals

news

By Staff Reporter · September 12, 2026

Police have arrested a suspect who allegedly spread a false rumour that OPay was shutting down its Nigerian operations, triggering panic withdrawals by thousands of customers across the country.

The Nigeria Police Force has arrested a suspect who allegedly spread a false rumour on social media that fintech platform OPay was shutting down its Nigerian operations, triggering panic withdrawals by thousands of customers.

The Force Public Relations Officer, CSP Ani Iniedu, said the suspect was tracked through digital forensic analysis after the rumour went viral on WhatsApp, X and TikTok, causing long queues at OPay agent points and a surge in withdrawal requests that temporarily strained the platform's infrastructure.

OPay, which serves over 40 million Nigerians and processes billions of naira in daily transactions, issued a statement denying the shutdown claim and assuring customers that their funds were safe. The company called on users to rely only on official communications. The police warned that spreading false financial rumours capable of causing public panic is a criminal offence under the Cybercrimes Act 2024 and carries severe penalties.
