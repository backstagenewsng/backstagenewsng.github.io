INEC Confirms Tinubu, Atiku, Obi on Final List of 19 Presidential Candidates for 2027

news

By Staff Reporter · September 13, 2026

INEC has published the final list of 19 presidential candidates for the January 16, 2027 election, with Tinubu (APC), Atiku (ADC), Obi (NDC) and Makinde (APM) among those confirmed.

The Independent National Electoral Commission has published the final list of 19 presidential candidates cleared to contest the January 16, 2027 presidential election, officially setting the stage for one of Nigeria's most competitive races.

President Bola Tinubu of the All Progressives Congress leads the list of confirmed candidates, alongside former Vice President Atiku Abubakar (African Democratic Congress), former Anambra Governor Peter Obi (Nigeria Democratic Congress) and Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde (Allied Peoples' Movement). The list also includes candidates from 15 other registered political parties.

INEC Chairman Prof. Mahmood Yakubu said the commission had complied with Section 32(1) of the Electoral Act 2026 by publishing the list within the statutory timeline. He noted that some ongoing court cases challenging candidate substitutions could still affect the final composition of the list, but that the commission would abide by all valid court orders. The presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for January 16, 2027.
