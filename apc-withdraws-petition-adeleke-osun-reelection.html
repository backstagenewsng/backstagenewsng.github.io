APC Withdraws Petition Challenging Adeleke's Re-election Victory in Osun

news

By Staff Reporter · September 9, 2026

The All Progressives Congress has withdrawn its petition challenging Governor Ademola Adeleke's re-election victory in the September 2025 Osun State governorship election, ending the legal challenge.

The All Progressives Congress has withdrawn its election petition challenging the re-election of Governor Ademola Adeleke in the September 2025 Osun State governorship election, effectively ending its legal challenge to the result.

The APC candidate, Gboyega Oyetola, had contested the outcome of the election, which Adeleke won by a decisive margin, extending his first-term mandate. The withdrawal was filed at the Osun State Governorship Election Petition Tribunal and comes amid speculation that the party's national leadership has decided to focus its resources on the 2027 general elections rather than continue litigating state-level disputes.

The development was welcomed by Adeleke's supporters, who described it as a vindication of the governor's mandate. PDP sources said the withdrawal confirmed that the election was free, fair and reflected the will of the people of Osun State.
