Tinubu Challenges Banks to Deploy More Capital to Productive Businesses

business

By Staff Reporter · September 9, 2026

President Tinubu has urged Nigerian banks to move beyond balance-sheet expansion and profitability by channeling more capital to productive businesses that drive investment and job creation.

President Bola Tinubu has challenged Nigerian banks to move beyond record profits and balance-sheet expansion by deploying more capital to productive businesses capable of driving investment, job creation and economic growth.

Tinubu spoke at the opening of the 19th Annual Banking and Finance Conference of the Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN) in Abuja, where he noted that the banking sector had reported impressive profits but that a significant portion of those gains came from foreign exchange revaluation and government securities rather than lending to the real economy.

The President urged the CIBN and the banking industry to work with the Central Bank of Nigeria to ensure that the ongoing financial sector reforms translate into tangible benefits for businesses and ordinary Nigerians. He said the government's reform agenda, including the unified foreign exchange market and subsidy removal, had stabilised the macroeconomic environment and that banks must now play their part by supporting agriculture, manufacturing and technology startups.
