NCAA Fines RwandAir N15 Million Over Operational Violations in Nigeria

business

By Staff Reporter · September 11, 2026

The Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority has fined RwandAir N15 million for operational violations in Nigeria, as the regulator intensifies enforcement of aviation standards.

The Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA) has slapped a N15 million fine on RwandAir for operational violations committed during its services to and from Nigeria.

The NCAA said the penalty followed an investigation into infractions related to the airline's operational conduct in Nigerian airspace and at Nigerian airports. The regulator said the fine was necessary to enforce compliance with Nigeria's aviation regulations and ensure the safety and reliability of air transport services.

RwandAir is among several international carriers that have faced NCAA sanctions in recent years as the authority steps up its oversight of foreign airlines operating in Nigerian airspace. The NCAA has warned that airlines that fail to comply with Nigerian aviation regulations will face further penalties, including the possible suspension of their operating permits.
