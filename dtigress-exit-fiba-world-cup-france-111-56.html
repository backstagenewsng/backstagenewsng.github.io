D'Tigress Crash Out of FIBA World Cup After 111-56 Thrashing by France

sports

By Staff Reporter · September 7, 2026

Nigeria's D'Tigress ended their 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup campaign without a win after France set a tournament record of 22 three-pointers in a dominant 111-56 victory in Berlin.

Nigeria's D'Tigress ended their 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup campaign on a deeply disappointing note after suffering a heavy 111-56 defeat to France in their final Group B match at the Berlin Arena on Monday.

France dominated from the opening tip, racing to a 31-13 first-quarter lead before extending their advantage to 65-26 at half-time. The European side set a new FIBA Women's World Cup record by converting 22 three-pointers in the match, with Gabby Williams leading the attack with 25 points and six rebounds. Grace Efosa-Aguebor was Nigeria's top scorer with 15 points off the bench.

The defeat was D'Tigress' third consecutive loss in Group B, following earlier defeats to South Korea (99-81) and Hungary (71-67), leaving the reigning five-time African champions bottom of the group and eliminated. Head coach Rena Wakama acknowledged that the team must rebuild, saying the World Cup exposed areas that need significant improvement. D'Tigress had arrived in Germany with high expectations after making history at the Paris 2024 Olympics as the first African team to reach the women's basketball quarter-finals.
