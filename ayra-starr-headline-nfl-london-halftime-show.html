Ayra Starr Makes History as First Afrobeats Artist to Headline NFL Halftime Show

entertainment

By Staff Reporter · September 7, 2026

Nigerian singer Ayra Starr will headline the halftime show at the 2026 NFL London Game between the Philadelphia Eagles and Jacksonville Jaguars on October 11, becoming the first Afrobeats artist to headline an NFL international game.

Nigerian Afrobeats star Ayra Starr will headline the halftime show at the 2026 NFL London Game between the Philadelphia Eagles and the Jacksonville Jaguars on October 11 at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, becoming the first Afrobeats artist to headline an NFL international game.

The two-time Grammy nominee, who has amassed over seven billion global streams and one billion YouTube views since her 2021 debut, said she was thrilled at the opportunity. "London is one of my favourite places in the world and to bring Starrgirl to the NFL global stage is a dream come true for me," she said.

The performance will be broadcast live in the UK on Channel 5 and Sky Sports. It is part of a record seven halftime shows across the 2026 NFL International Games, spanning seven countries and four continents. The NFL described Starr as a "generational talent" and said the decision reflects the growing influence of African music on the global stage. Starr released her third studio album, Starrgirl, on August 14 via Mavin Records and Republic Records.
