Governorship and State Assembly Campaigns Open Nationwide for 2027 Elections

news

By Staff Reporter · September 9, 2026

Partisan activities surged across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones as governorship and state House of Assembly candidates officially kicked off their campaigns for the February 6, 2027 elections.

Partisan activities surged across Nigeria's six geopolitical zones on Tuesday, September 9, as governorship and state House of Assembly candidates officially kicked off their campaigns for the February 6, 2027 elections, in line with the timetable released by the Independent National Electoral Commission.

The opening of campaigns unleashed a wave of rallies, town hall meetings and door-to-door canvassing as candidates scrambled to introduce themselves to voters and outline their platforms. In several states, incumbent governors seeking re-election staged large rallies, while opposition candidates held smaller, community-level events.

INEC Chairman Prof. Mahmood Yakubu reminded all political parties and candidates that campaign activities must comply with the Electoral Act 2026, including the ban on vote-buying, hate speech and the use of state resources for campaigns. He warned that violators would face sanctions, including possible disqualification. The governorship and assembly elections are scheduled for February 6, 2027, three weeks after the presidential and National Assembly elections on January 16.
