Dangote Says Refinery Shares Could Rise From N525 to N10,000

business

By Staff Reporter · September 13, 2026

Aliko Dangote has told potential investors that shares in the Dangote Refinery, offered at N525 in the upcoming IPO, could rise to as high as N10,000 based on the company's growth trajectory and expansion plans.

Africa's richest man, Aliko Dangote, has told potential investors that shares in the Dangote Petroleum Refinery, offered at N525 per share in the upcoming IPO, could rise to as high as N10,000 based on the company's growth trajectory and planned capacity expansion.

Speaking ahead of the IPO's opening on September 14, Dangote said the refinery's current production of 650,000 barrels per day was only the beginning, and that the planned doubling of capacity to 1.4 million barrels per day, combined with petrochemical and fertiliser operations, would transform the company's earnings and share value over the next decade.

He urged ordinary Nigerians to participate in the IPO, noting that the minimum investment of 10 shares at N5,250 was deliberately set low to allow broad participation. The IPO includes a bonus of one free share for every 10 shares held continuously for 12 months. Dangote said the refinery had already raised $750 million through a Eurobond and secured a $1 billion underwriting facility, demonstrating strong institutional confidence in the company's future.
