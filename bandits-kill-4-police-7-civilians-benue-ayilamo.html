Bandits Kill Four Police Officers and Seven Civilians in Seven-Hour Benue Attack

news

By Staff Reporter · September 11, 2026

Suspected bandits attacked Ayilamo community in Logo LGA, Benue State, killing four police officers and seven civilians in a gun battle that lasted about seven hours before security forces repelled the attackers.

Suspected bandits attacked Ayilamo community in Logo Local Government Area of Benue State on Wednesday evening, killing four police officers and seven civilians in a fierce gun battle that lasted approximately seven hours.

The Benue State Police Command said the attackers invaded the community at about 6:12 p.m. on September 10. Officers from the Police Mobile Force, tactical teams and conventional police engaged the assailants in a sustained firefight. According to a statement by the command's spokesperson, Orchia Aondongu, the operatives stood firm in defence of the community and successfully repelled the attack, forcing the criminals to retreat into the surrounding forest with heavy casualties in the early hours of September 11.

The attack came about a year after the IGP upgraded the Ayilamo Police Station to a divisional headquarters as part of a broader restructuring of policing in Benue. Governor Hyacinth Alia said the DSS had apprehended some of those behind the attack and commiserated with the victims' families.
