Nollywood Actress Atinuke Kareem Dies of Breast Cancer

entertainment

By Staff Reporter · September 2, 2026

Nollywood actress Temitope Osoba, popularly known as Atinuke Kareem, has died of breast cancer, barely eight days after the death of veteran actor Taiwo Hassan (Ogogo), plunging the Nigerian film industry into fresh mourning.

Nollywood actress Temitope Osoba, popularly known by her screen name Atinuke Kareem, has died after a battle with breast cancer, plunging Nigeria's film industry into fresh mourning barely eight days after the death of veteran actor Taiwo Hassan, popularly known as Ogogo.

Colleagues in the Yoruba film industry confirmed her death and paid tributes on social media, describing her as a talented and dedicated actress who brought warmth and authenticity to her roles. She was known for her work in several popular Yoruba-language films.

Her burial was held at a newly completed house in Iperu Remo, Ogun State, which she had reportedly finished building shortly before her death and was preparing to celebrate with family and friends. Videos of the funeral, showing mourners gathered at the residence, circulated widely on social media. Her death adds to the string of losses that have hit the Nigerian entertainment industry in recent months.
