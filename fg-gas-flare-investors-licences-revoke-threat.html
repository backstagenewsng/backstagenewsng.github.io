FG Threatens to Revoke Licences of Gas Flare Investors Who Fail to Develop Projects

business

By Staff Reporter · September 10, 2026

The Federal Government has threatened to revoke the licences of investors that fail to develop projects awarded under its gas flare commercialisation programme, as oil producers continue to flare significant volumes of natural gas.

The Federal Government has threatened to revoke the licences of investors that have failed to develop projects awarded under the gas flare commercialisation programme, as Nigeria's efforts to eliminate gas flaring enter a critical phase.

Oil producers continue to flare significant volumes of natural gas in the Niger Delta despite years of government initiatives aimed at capturing the gas for domestic power generation, industrial use and export. The gas flare commercialisation programme, managed by the Nigerian Upstream Petroleum Regulatory Commission, was designed to award flare sites to third-party investors who would capture and commercialise the gas.

Officials said several licence holders had made no visible progress on their awarded sites and warned that failure to show evidence of development within the next compliance window would result in licence revocation and reallocation to more capable investors. Nigeria ranks among the world's top gas-flaring nations, with the practice costing the economy billions of dollars in lost revenue and contributing significantly to greenhouse gas emissions.
