Troops Kill 18 Terrorists at Meeting Point in Borno State

news

By Staff Reporter · September 11, 2026

Nigerian military troops have killed at least 18 suspected terrorists during an operation that targeted a gathering point in Borno State, recovering weapons and ammunition.

Nigerian military troops operating under Operation Hadin Kai have killed at least 18 suspected terrorists during a coordinated operation that targeted a known gathering point in Borno State.

Military sources said the operation, carried out in the early hours of Thursday, was based on intelligence that a group of ISWAP and Boko Haram fighters had assembled at a location in the Lake Chad axis for a planning meeting. Ground troops, supported by air assets, surrounded and engaged the terrorists, who were caught off guard.

Weapons recovered from the scene included AK-47 rifles, rocket-propelled grenade launchers, improvised explosive device components and a large quantity of ammunition. The Defence Headquarters said the operation demonstrated the military's commitment to sustaining pressure on terrorist groups and denying them the space to regroup, plan or launch attacks against civilian communities.
