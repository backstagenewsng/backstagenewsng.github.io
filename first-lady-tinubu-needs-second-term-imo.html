First Lady Says Tinubu Administration Needs Second Term to Complete Its Plans

news

By Staff Reporter · September 10, 2026

Nigeria's First Lady Oluremi Tinubu has urged Nigerians to support President Tinubu's re-election, saying the government needs another four years to complete its development agenda.

Nigeria's First Lady, Senator Oluremi Tinubu, has called on Nigerians to continue supporting President Bola Tinubu's administration, saying the government needs another four-year term to complete its plans for the country.

Speaking during her visit to Imo State, where she addressed supporters and Catholic women's groups, the First Lady said the administration had hit the ground running immediately after taking office and was making progress on critical areas including infrastructure, security and economic reform.

She urged women in particular to mobilise support for the President's re-election bid, describing him as a leader who was committed to improving the lives of ordinary Nigerians despite inheriting what she called a broken economy. The First Lady also donated supplies to women's groups in the state during the visit.
