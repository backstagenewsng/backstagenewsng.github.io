Dangote Refinery IPO Opens: Investors Commit N10 Billion in First Hour

business

By Staff Reporter · September 14, 2026

Aliko Dangote rang the opening bell at the Nigerian Exchange as the Dangote Refinery IPO launched, with investors committing over N10 billion in the first hour of Africa's largest-ever share sale.

Africa's richest man, Aliko Dangote, rang the opening bell at the Nigerian Exchange on Monday to launch what is set to become Africa's largest-ever initial public offering, with investors committing more than N10 billion ($7 million) within the first hour of trading.

The IPO offers 4.1 billion new ordinary shares at N525 per share, with a minimum subscription of 10 shares valued at N5,250. The offer is targeted at retail, institutional and eligible African investors, and will remain open until October 13, 2026. The transaction is available through 40 approved platforms and stockbrokers.

Dangote said the IPO was about more than raising capital. "This is about creating an opportunity for ordinary Nigerians, Africans and investors across the globe to participate directly in one of the most transformative industrial projects ever built on the continent," he said. The IPO includes a bonus of one free share for every 10 shares purchased and held for 12 consecutive months. While N10 billion in the first hour is significant, it represents less than 0.5 percent of the N2.15 trillion target, meaning the real test of the offer's success will play out over the coming weeks.
