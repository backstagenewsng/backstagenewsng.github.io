Akpabio Denies Saying Northerners Would Suffer if Tinubu Loses 2027 Election

news

By Staff Reporter · September 10, 2026

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has dismissed as a complete fabrication claims that he said northerners would suffer if President Tinubu fails to win re-election in 2027.

Senate President Godswill Akpabio has dismissed as a complete fabrication claims that he told a meeting in the North that northerners would suffer if President Bola Tinubu fails to win re-election in 2027.

In a statement issued on Wednesday by his Special Adviser on Media and Publicity, Hon. Eseme Eyiboh, Akpabio described the alleged statement as a calculated attempt to damage his reputation and cause division between northern and southern Nigerians ahead of the January 2027 election.

He challenged the alleged sponsor of the material to produce evidence of when and where he made such a statement, adding that as a ranking national leader, he had always promoted unity and inclusiveness. The Senate President said he would not be drawn into political mudslinging and urged Nigerians to reject propaganda from any quarter.
