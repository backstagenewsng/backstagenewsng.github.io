Auditor-General Flags N124 Billion in Irregular Contracts Across Federal MDAs

business

By Staff Reporter · September 13, 2026

The Auditor-General of the Federation has flagged N124 billion in contracts awarded across multiple ministries, departments and agencies without proper documentation or due process.

The Auditor-General of the Federation has flagged approximately N124 billion in contracts awarded across multiple federal ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs) without proper documentation, due process or evidence of completion.

The findings, contained in the Auditor-General's latest report submitted to the National Assembly, revealed cases of contracts awarded without competitive bidding, payments made for projects with no evidence of execution, and transfers to unverified beneficiaries. The report covers the 2024 and 2025 fiscal years and names several high-profile agencies.

The Senate Committee on Public Accounts is expected to summon the accounting officers of the affected MDAs for questioning. The Auditor-General recommended the recovery of all funds disbursed without proper authorisation and the prosecution of officials found to have violated the Financial Regulations and the Procurement Act. This is the second major audit red flag in recent weeks, after an earlier report flagged N33.75 billion in transfers to unverified beneficiaries.
