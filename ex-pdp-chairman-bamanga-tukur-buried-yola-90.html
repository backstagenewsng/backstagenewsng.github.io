Ex-PDP Chairman Bamanga Tukur Buried in Yola at 90 as Political Leaders Mourn

news

By Staff Reporter · September 13, 2026

Former PDP national chairman and ex-Governor of Gongola State, Alhaji Bamanga Tukur, has been buried in Yola after funeral prayers at the Lamido of Adamawa's Palace Mosque, with Dangote and governors in attendance.

Former Peoples Democratic Party national chairman and ex-Governor of the defunct Gongola State, Alhaji Bamanga Muhammad Tukur, was buried on Sunday, September 13, 2026, at his residence in Yola, Adamawa State, after funeral prayers at the Lamido of Adamawa's Palace Mosque.

The Janazah prayer was led by Islamic clerics at 11:00 a.m., with a massive turnout of mourners, government officials and dignitaries. Africa's richest man, Aliko Dangote, was among the prominent attendees, alongside Adamawa Governor Ahmadu Umaru Fintiri, who declared three days of mourning in the state, and former Governor Umar Jibrilla Bindow.

President Tinubu, northern governors, PDP leaders, the Senate and the House of Representatives all issued tributes describing Tukur as a distinguished patriot, an elder statesman and an industrialist who dedicated his life to national unity and development. Tukur, who died on Saturday at 90, served as PDP chairman from 2012 to 2014 and was a founding member of Nigeria's post-independence business class.
