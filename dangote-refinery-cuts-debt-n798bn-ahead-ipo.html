Dangote Refinery Cuts Secured Debt by N798bn Ahead of IPO

business

By Staff Reporter · September 10, 2026

The Dangote Petroleum Refinery cut its secured debt by approximately N798 billion in the first half of 2026, bringing outstanding obligations down to about N7.9 trillion as it ramps production ahead of its landmark IPO.

The Dangote Petroleum Refinery and Petrochemicals cut its secured debt by approximately N798 billion ($570 million) in the first half of 2026, bringing its outstanding obligations down to roughly N7.9 trillion ($5.67 billion) as the 700,000-barrel-per-day facility ramped up production and strengthened cash generation ahead of its planned initial public offering.

The debt reduction came as the refinery increased crude throughput and expanded its export operations, with Nigeria's seaborne refined petroleum exports to Europe reaching record levels. The company's improving financial profile is seen as a key factor in building investor confidence ahead of the IPO, which the Securities and Exchange Commission approved last week.

The IPO, which is expected to open on September 14 at N525 per share, could raise up to N2.15 trillion and become Africa's biggest-ever share sale. Aliko Dangote has said proceeds will fund the refinery's planned expansion from 650,000 to 1.4 million barrels per day.
