Three Killed, 10 Injured in Multiple Crash on Lagos-Ibadan Expressway

news

By Staff Reporter · September 4, 2026

Three women were killed and 10 others injured after a commercial bus, a Toyota bus and a car collided on the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway near Long Bridge, with the FRSC blaming excessive speed.

Three women were killed and 10 others sustained injuries in a multiple road crash involving three vehicles on the Lagos-Ibadan Expressway in Ogun State on Friday evening, September 4, 2026.

The Federal Road Safety Corps said the crash occurred at about 5:10pm at the Long Bridge, opposite Mikano, involving a white Mazda commercial bus, a white Toyota bus and a white Toyota Corolla car. Sixteen people were involved in total, comprising 12 males and four females. Ten people sustained injuries while three female victims were confirmed dead at the scene.

The FRSC attributed the crash to speed violation and loss of control. The remains of the deceased were evacuated to Ifo Morgue and Idera Morgue in Sagamu, while the three damaged vehicles were handed over to the Nigeria Police Force at Warewa for investigation. Ogun State Sector Commander Oludare Ogunjobi urged motorists to exercise maximum caution and avoid excessive speed on the expressway.
