Nigerian Equities Market Loses N1.96 Trillion in One Week as Bearish Sentiment Prevails

business

By Staff Reporter · September 12, 2026

The Nigerian stock market shed N1.96 trillion in market capitalisation over the past week as investors took profits amid concerns over global oil prices and the upcoming Dangote Refinery IPO.

The Nigerian equities market closed the week ended September 12 with a cumulative loss of N1.96 trillion in market capitalisation, as bearish sentiment prevailed across most sectors.

The All-Share Index fell by 3.2 percent over the five trading days to close at 103,412 points, driven by sell-offs in banking, oil and gas, and industrial stocks. Analysts attributed the decline to profit-taking by institutional investors who had accumulated positions in the first half of the year, as well as caution ahead of the Dangote Refinery IPO which opens on September 14.

Market watchers said the sell-off was also influenced by a dip in global crude oil prices, which fell below $72 per barrel mid-week before recovering slightly. Analysts at Chapel Hill Denham said the market was entering a consolidation phase and expected renewed buying interest once the Dangote IPO pricing becomes clearer and Q3 earnings reports begin to filter in from October.
