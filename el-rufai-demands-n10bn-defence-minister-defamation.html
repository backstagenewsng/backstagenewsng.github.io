El-Rufai Demands N10bn From Defence Minister Over Alleged Defamation

news

By Staff Reporter · September 8, 2026

Former Kaduna Governor Nasir El-Rufai has demanded N10 billion compensation and a public apology from Defence Minister Christopher Musa over remarks accusing him of planning killings in Southern Kaduna.

Former Kaduna State Governor Nasir El-Rufai has demanded N10 billion in compensation and a public apology from the Minister of Defence, General Christopher Musa (retd.), over alleged defamatory statements made during a television interview.

The demand was contained in a letter before action dated Monday, September 7, 2026, issued by El-Rufai's lawyers, Chambers of Ubong Akpan. The legal threat followed Musa's appearance on Channels Television's Politics Today on September 3, where the minister accused El-Rufai of deliberately planning the killing of people in Southern Kaduna, paying bandits, and demolishing the properties of political opponents while he was governor.

El-Rufai's lawyers described the statements as false and defamatory, arguing they portrayed their client as a criminal, a murderer and a sponsor of banditry. They gave the minister seven days to retract the statements, publish an apology in two national newspapers, and pay the N10 billion compensation, or face legal proceedings. The Middle Belt Forum has however backed the minister, saying his claims are true and urging him to stand by his words.
